GUIDE / 01

What Continuum is#

Continuum is a desktop home for AI Agents that research, build, use tools and carry work forward across conversations and scheduled jobs — with your models and files.

Continuum gives AI work a place to continue. Start a conversation with Main or a specialist Agent, bring several Agents into a group, and work with real files, repositories and connected services. Agents can research a question, change code, create a presentation, build an editable design or explain data interactively. Useful methods become Skills; missing tools can become reusable Agent apps.

The app and its execution engine run on your machine, and the desktop host stores and executes work locally. Requests go to the models and services you configure, which may be hosted providers or local model servers — so local storage does not imply that every operation stays offline.

GUIDE / 02

A typical session#

  1. Choose an Agent. Open New chat, pick Main or a specialist, and attach the files, references or apps the work needs. The conversation keeps its selected Agent.
  2. Describe the result. Ask for research, a code change, a deck, a visualization or an editable design. Follow tool activity and review the output as it arrives.
  3. Keep what works. Reopen saved outputs, turn a method into a Skill, or reuse an Agent app instead of describing the same tool again.
  4. Give ongoing work a home. Use the clock in the navigation rail to plan scheduled work. Review the proposed outcome, settings and permissions, then manage it in Activity.
  5. Return to the result. Inspect progress and evidence, resume interrupted work, or ask a conversation to explain a job's saved outcome and data.

GUIDE / 03

Choose your models and keep control of access#

Connect providers such as OpenAI and Anthropic, compatible endpoints, or local servers such as Ollama and LM Studio. Configure each Agent's model and the available thinking and vision settings. Tools and model capabilities depend on the selected provider, model and configuration.

Permissions are enforced by the desktop host. File access, shell operations, connected tools, app secrets and computer control follow their relevant grants and approval rules. Review the requested access, keep useful capabilities enabled, and change or revoke grants when your needs change.

An installation can be restricted by a managed configuration: it can limit adding new providers, MCP servers, Agent apps, shell commands or computer use while leaving existing resources usable, editable and removable. A capability a policy withdraws is removed from what the model can call rather than left in place to fail.

GUIDE / 04

Agents and groups#

Main is an editable built-in Agent that can help you find and understand your saved chats, scheduled work and results. Create other Agents for the work you repeat: a researcher, a release engineer, a writer or a product reviewer. Give each a model and standing instructions so your preferred approach travels with the Agent.

With memory enabled, an Agent can retain useful facts, decisions and procedures across its work. Its private memories and learned Skills belong to that Agent. Settings lets you inspect what it has learned, follow its sources and activity, review recorded job outcomes, and forget selected memories or clear its memory.

Group chats give Main and your specialists a shared transcript. Main coordinates assignments; Agents report progress and ask questions where you can see them. Address a member with an @mention and follow who is working or waiting.

Choose Incognito when creating a chat to exclude it from cross-chat knowledge and Agent learning. The conversation remains in local history. Incognito is a knowledge boundary, not a promise of anonymous requests to an external provider.

GUIDE / 05

Scheduled work that keeps its progress#

Describe an outcome and let Continuum draft a plan with criteria, an execution approach and notification preferences. Use it for a job you run once or work that repeats, such as collecting data, checking a condition or preparing a regular brief. Advanced settings let you adjust the Agent, strategy, schedule and constraints.

Runs record progress, evidence, budget use and checkpoints. They can wait for your input and resume supported interrupted work. Execution budgets remain visible, and extensions require your decision. Notifications can call attention to completion or meaningful changes.

Find jobs in Activity › Scheduled work. Search the collection, run or resume a job, stop its work and schedule, open its conversation, or archive it. Compact cards show status and a result preview; expand a job's instructions, configuration and run activity when you need the detail.

Ask about saved outcomes from a conversation. For a compatible completed result, Explore result starts a linked conversation with a concise record of the job's objective, outcome and evidence, preserving its Agent and model selection.

A job you run once is as valid as a recurring one — a schedule is optional. Scheduled execution runs through the desktop host, so keep it running for jobs to execute, and make sure the connected sources, credentials and permissions a job needs are still available.

GUIDE / 06

What you can create#

Research and coding

Gather sources, compare findings and produce a report with evidence you can inspect. For code, work against a real folder or repository: inspect files, make changes, run commands and tests, and review the result. The in-app browser can sit beside the conversation for browsing and previewing work.

“Find why this test fails, fix the cause, run the relevant checks and show me what changed.”

Presentations and interactive explanations

Build a deck from ready source material, or ask for research and an outline to review first. Present it or export to PPTX, and save a visual style for future decks. Ask for an interactive visualization when a chart, slider, simulation or comparison would make an idea easier to understand.

Outputs stay connected to their conversation: Outputs, Tools, Preview and Activity share the right sidebar, so you can inspect a file, use a live app card or browse a page while the conversation stays available. Reopen or export supported outputs, and use Show in folder for files that belong in another application.

GUIDE / 07

The visual design editor#

Open Design to work on a canvas with the Designer Agent beside you. Create and edit shapes, text, layouts and components through direct manipulation or conversation, and keep the result as an editable design file.

Design systems save tokens, typography, components and examples, so later work reuses those visual foundations instead of rebuilding the brief each time.

Keep refining the result. Select layers on the canvas, adjust their size and appearance, and work with reusable components. Use the prototype controls to connect screens with actions.

GUIDE / 08

Talk through the work, or let the Agent operate it#

Live brings voice, screen sharing and spoken replies into the same conversation. Choose the channels you need, type when convenient, and use the compact Live card while keeping another application in view. Screen-assisted turns require a configured vision-capable model.

The Agent can use browser tools and connected services. Computer use adds screen inspection and mouse or keyboard actions when the work requires them. Real computer input uses a distinct permission handover; in Live, that approval lasts for the session. You can interrupt, and control is released when the session ends.

“Look at this screen, explain what needs changing, and help me make the edit.”

Mobile app testing

For developers, mobile app testing connects to a configured Appium server to operate Android emulators or iOS Simulators and collect screenshot and assertion evidence. Android needs its emulator and drivers; iOS Simulator needs a Mac with Xcode. This integration has mock coverage, so native device acceptance must be verified separately. Configuration lives in the repository's mobile emulator testing setup guide.

GUIDE / 09

Extend the way your Agents work#

UseWhen you need itExample request
Skills Reusable knowledge and instructions, optionally invoked with a slash command. “Save our release review method as /release-audit.”
Agent apps A persistent tool with code, service access and an optional interactive UI. “Build a tool that queries our analytics API and charts retention.”
MCP connections Tools supplied by a connected server. “Connect this MCP server so we can use its tools here.”
Workflows A saved sequence of actions, decisions and checks. “Create a release workflow that stops when verification fails.”

Skills capture a method. Agent apps provide a tool. Workflows record a process. Scheduled work gives an objective an execution history and, when needed, a cadence. You can combine them without losing the original conversation or saved result. Agent apps can use approved network, file, shell and secret access, including the dependencies their work needs; global apps are available across chats, and you control app enablement and capability grants.

GUIDE / 10

For builders: an extensible agent harness#

Under the desktop experience is a shared execution engine for conversations and durable jobs. Eight strategies support interactive work, coding, planning with workers, research, monitoring, computer use, saved workflows and artifact building. Tools, capabilities and model providers can be extended without creating a separate execution system for every feature.

  • interactive — the everyday conversation loop.
  • coding — work against a repository with verification.
  • planner-worker — a queue of bounded stages with checkpoints.
  • workflow — execution of a saved workflow graph.
  • research — sourced investigation.
  • monitoring — repeated checks of a condition.
  • computer-use — screen inspection and real input.
  • artifact-build — producing a durable artifact.

We describe Continuum as the open AI harness. Paired with the plain explanation: Continuum connects your models, tools and files, and manages how Agents use them to carry out work.

A Mission is the engineering name for what the interface calls scheduled work, and the shared run kernel is the single executor for durable runs. Conversation streaming stays in the chat engine; durable orchestration belongs to the run kernel, with no third loop, and a Workspace remains a composition of a conversation, jobs and apps rather than another executor.

GUIDE / 11

What to keep in mind#

  • Local storage is not offline execution. The model and services you choose determine where requests are processed.
  • Setup sits next to the capability. Scheduled work needs the desktop host running; screen-assisted Live turns need a configured vision-capable model; iOS Simulator testing needs a Mac with Xcode.
  • Provider access, external services and local runtimes have their own requirements and their own usage terms. Continuum does not remove model costs.
  • Capabilities vary by model. Thinking and vision settings are per-model configuration, not guarantees.
  • Grants stay yours. App capability grants, computer-control handovers and scheduled-work permissions can be changed or revoked at any time.
  • Examples describe work you can request — not guaranteed outcomes. You review plans and results as they arrive.

GUIDE / 12

The one-paragraph version#

Continuum is a desktop app for working with AI Agents across research, coding, design and automation. Choose your models, shape specialist Agents, and connect the files and services they need. Create reports, presentations, editable designs and interactive outputs in conversation. Build reusable tools and Skills, or plan scheduled work that records progress and results you can return to. Inspect the work, manage access, and decide when to continue or stop.
